Order Processing Steps:

  • Browsing: Owner searches and browses supplier products
  • Cart Management: Add/remove items and adjust quantities
  • Checkout: Review order details and select payment method
  • Order Creation: System creates order record with initial status
  • Supplier Processing: Supplier prepares and ships the order
  • Delivery: Owner receives order and verifies payment
  • Completion: Order marked as complete and notifications sent

  • GCash: Mobile payment through GCash platform
  • PayMaya: Online payment through PayMaya platform
  • Bank Transfer: Direct bank-to-bank transfer
  • Verification: Supplier verifies payment before marking order complete
  • Reminders: System sends reminders for pending payments

Key Data Models:

  • User: Django authentication model for all users
  • UserProfile: Extended user info with role and contact details
  • SupplierProfile: Supplier-specific business information
  • Inventory: Store owner's inventory items
  • SupplierProduct: Products offered by suppliers
  • Order: Purchase orders from owners to suppliers
  • Payment: Payment records and verification status
  • Notification: System notifications for users
  • Message: Direct messages between users
